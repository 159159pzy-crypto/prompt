质感与氛围标签库（模板 §13，槽位 [detail/mood]，灯光/光影/色调禁止输出）
画面质感（选 1 个，除非刻意混搭不超 2 种）：水墨风 ink splash, ink wash, calligraphic brushstrokes, watercolor texture（古风/和风）；漫画风 comic-style, halftone dots, screentone patterns, comic, greyscale（差分/对比/恶堕）；素描风 sketch, lineart, scribbly shading；绘画风 painterly；胶片风 film grain, vintage film grain, heavy film grain overlay, emulsion scratch（怀旧/偷拍）；黑白 greyscale, black and white, monochrome, spot color, limited palette（压抑/艺术化）。
运动渲染（选 1 个，最多叠 2 个）：motion lines（漫画式动作线，361次高频）；speed lines（方向性速度线）；motion blur（摄影级运动模糊）；multiple overlapping motion blurs（残影叠加）；afterimages, afterimages due to excessive speed。适用：后入/骑乘/种付/传教士冲刺阶段。
光学/摄影效果：depth of field, shallow depth of field, bokeh（虚化背景）；silhouette, backlit silhouette（透过门窗/磨砂玻璃）；lens flare, lens flare streaks（镜头光晕，浪漫）；bloom（高光溢出，梦幻/恍惚）；soft focus（柔和朦胧，事后温存）；chromatic aberration（色差，赛博/故障/催眠）；vignette（边缘压暗）；multiple exposure effect（重叠影像，回忆/幻象）。
数字/故障效果（选 1 个，最多叠 2 个）：digital glitch effects, glitch art（赛博/催眠/崩坏）；VHS distortion, tracking errors, scan lines, CRT scanlines（偷拍/录像带）；pixelated outlines, blocky pixelated texture（遮挡/像素风）；data stream effects, binary code particles（赛博/催眠 app）。日常性爱不用。
氛围基调（选 1 个）：cinematic, cinematic composition, cinematic angle（封面/主视觉）；dramatic tension, dramatic shadows（过激/胁迫/对峙）；ethereal, dreamcore, dreamlike（浮空/幻想/恍惚高潮）；dark atmosphere, suspenseful, ominous（地牢/过激/恶堕）；chiaroscuro（经典油画光）；poetic atmosphere（古风/和风/事后）；chaos, explosive composition（群交/过激/战败）。
§13.6 禁止输出清单（绝对禁止出现在正面与负面 Token，LoRA 已内置光影）：光线描述 sunlight, moonlight, dim light, candlelight, neon light, neon lights, streetlights；光影技术 backlighting, rim light, warm lighting, cool lighting, golden hour glow, soft lighting；色调描述 warm tone, cool tone, sepia, blue tone, amber tone；光学现象 god rays, light rays, light particles, volumetric light beams, tyndall effect；发光描述 glowing, illuminated, lit, backlit, spotlight, flash。允许：环境天气（rain, snow, fog, steam, stormy, dust particles, underwater）与时辰/大气标签。
